# Pasta puttanesca
## Ingredients
* 4 slices of bacon or 4 oz of pancetta
* 1 can anchovies
* 1 small yellow onion, diced
* 3-4 cloves of garlic, chopped
* 1 28oz can of peeled san marzano tomatoes
* 25 kalamata olives
* 10 manzanilla olives
* 1 T capers
* chili flakes
* 4-5 basil leaves
* 1 lb of pasta
* salt

## Directions
* Start boiling water with salt and when it's ready, add the pasta to cook according to instructions.
* Cut the slices of bacon into thin strips and cook over medium heat in a pan large enough for the entire dish. Remove bacon from the pan.
* Cut anchovies into small pieces and add to the pan, along with diced onion. Add salt and start cooking the onions to soften them. Add the garlic after the onions have had time to cook for a bit. Anchovies will largely disappear.
* Carefully crush the tomatoes and add them to the pan. They can spurt while crushing.
* Cut all of the olives in half and add to the tomatoes. Add the capers and chili flakes as well.
* When the pasta is almost done, tear and add the basil to the tomatoes. Then, drain the pasta and add it to the sauce as well, and mix. Serves four.

## Notes
* Puttanesca can have whatever ingredients you want, but it's usually fairly strongly flavored ingredients (like the olives and capers). At a minimum I could keep the onions, garlic, and tomatoes.
* It's uncommon to have meat in it, so the bacon or pancetta is unusual. The anchovies give some depth of flavor without being fishy.
* A 12 inch pan is large enough for this dish.
