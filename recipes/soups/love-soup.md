# Love Soup

## Ingredients
* 1 lb ground beef
* 4 T beef bouillon
* 1/4 c dried minced onions
* 1/2 c dried split peas
* 1/2 c uncooked twist macaroni
* 1/4 c barley
* 1/2 c dried lentils
* 1/3 c long-grained white rice
* 1 c uncooked tri-color spiral pasta

## Directions
* In a large kettle, brown 1 pound ground beef in 2 tablespoons of cooking oil.
* Add 12 cups of water, along with all of the ingredients other than tri-color pasta.
* Bring to a boil and simmer 45 minutes.
* Add tri-color pasta and simmer 15 minutes more.
* Serve with bread or crackers.

## Notes
* The ground beef should be 85% lean / 15% fat. Cook it under low to medium heat so it doesn't burn. You will need to
  break up the ground beef while it is being browned.
* The 4 tablespoons of beef bouillon is equivalent to 12 teaspoons of beef bouillon or 12 beef bouilon cubes. If you
  cannot find beef bouillon, you can skip it and use beef stock instead of water.
